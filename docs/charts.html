Diatonic chords for every jam key

Open grips

First position. Other keys use an E-form or A-form barre.

Ten-dollar chords

6ths, maj7s, 9ths, m7s, a dim7, and the V of V.

Chromatics

Land on a chord tone, usually the root, when the chord hits.

Land on the root

Four chromatic notes. The last one is the root, on the downbeat.

From a fret below

Same closed shape, one fret lower, then slide up. F# into G, C# into D, G# into A.

From a fret above

Start a half step sharp and come down. Don’t sit on the upper fret.

Half-step slide

Closed E-form so the whole grip moves. Play the left diagram, slide to the middle. The right-hand grip is the fall-in from above.

Chromatic walk into the root

Four notes, last one is the chord root. Repeat it on any string that has that root.

Chord tones vs. approach notes

Teal is the target chord. Amber is a half step below each of those tones — slide or walk into the teal.

Scale charts

Capo fret by song key
